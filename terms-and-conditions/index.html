How you join the program

You join by submitting the sign-up form on the SMS Messaging page of this website: entering your mobile phone number and checking the SMS consent box. That form is the only way to sign up. The consent box is never checked for you, and checking it is entirely optional.

By signing up you agree to receive recurring SMS messages from Ruchie Landau Photography regarding booking confirmations, photography session reminders, scheduling changes, and booking-related customer support.

Messages you will receive

The program sends informational messages related to your photography booking only. It is not used for promotional or marketing messages.

Eligibility

The program is available on participating US and Canadian mobile carriers. You must be the subscriber of the mobile number you enroll, or be authorized by the subscriber to enroll it, and you must be able to receive text messages on that number.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any Ruchie Landau Photography text message to unsubscribe. After you send STOP, we will send one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will not receive further program messages. You can rejoin at any time by signing up again on this website.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any Ruchie Landau Photography text message for assistance. You can also email rhoffmanstudios@gmail.com or call 845‑213‑0776. Contacting us by email or phone is for support only and does not sign you up for text messages.

Carriers and delivery

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery depends on your carrier and on conditions outside our control, so we cannot guarantee that every message will be delivered or delivered on time.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Changing your number

If the mobile number you enrolled changes or is no longer yours, please reply STOP so that messages are not sent to the wrong person.
