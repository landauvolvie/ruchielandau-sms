SMS Information

What messages will I receive?

Booking confirmations, photography session reminders, scheduling changes, and customer-support messages related to your photography booking. The program is not used for promotional or marketing messages.

How often will I receive messages?

Message frequency varies based on your bookings and communications with Ruchie Landau Photography.

Does SMS cost anything?

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

How do I stop messages?

Reply STOP to any Ruchie Landau Photography SMS message to unsubscribe.

How do I get help?

Reply HELP to any Ruchie Landau Photography SMS message for assistance, or contact us at 845‑213‑0776 or rhoffmanstudios@gmail.com. Contacting us does not sign you up for text messages.

Is SMS required to book?

No. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing or booking photography services.
